Privacy Policy

Health Bot Tasks is a personal, single-user tool. It accesses only the owner's own Google Tasks (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) to list, create, complete, and delete tasks on the owner's behalf.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored in the owner's private Google Cloud Secret Manager. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
